Battleship

----

Start

Two players, one 10×10 grid each. Take a seat, place five ships, then trade shots.

  • Fleet: Carrier 6, Battleship 5, Cruiser 4, Submarine 3, Destroyer 2.
  • Ships can touch but not overlap, and must sit straight.
  • One shot per turn, players alternate. Sink all five to win.
